the loop

Build · ship · orbit

One platform from a commit to a rollout. No XML, no separate deploy tool, no vendor-locked registry.

Build

Kaniko, BuildKit, or Buildah — in-cluster, daemonless, rootless. OCI image + index out the other side, multi-arch by default.

Ship

Push via crane, continuously verified against the upstream OCI conformance suite. Signatures, SBOMs and provenance ride along as referrers.

Orbit

Promote dev → staging → production along a trajectory. Auto-promote or gate on a human. Per-target rollback when a deploy goes dark.
