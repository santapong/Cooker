faq

Before you launch

Is the free tier really free?

Yes. Explorer is the single-binary build, self-hosted, with unlimited pipelines and runs. You only pay when you need multi-replica HA, enterprise SSO, or managed secret backends.

What does "per replica" mean?

You're billed per running Cooker process, not per user or per pipeline. Seats are unlimited on every paid tier — invite the whole crew.

Can I bring my own registry?

Always. Cooker is built on go-containerregistry and verified against the OCI distribution-spec. No vendor lock-in, no proprietary registry.

Do I have to use OIDC?

No. In dev and UAT mode a dev admin is injected so you can start instantly. OIDC + PKCE is there when you're ready for production.
